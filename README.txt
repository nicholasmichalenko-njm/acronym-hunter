STATIONKEEPER (Portable, Offline)
=================================

SHRED: COMPLIANCE LANGUAGE ON ANY WORDS (4.18.0)
  - In Shred, drag over any words of a piece and right-click: Compliance language: just here (kept on the piece),
    Compliance language: everywhere (the words join the compliance words list every outline, matrix and setup reads),
    or Not compliance language here (a word the list marks, taken off this piece). Clear this piece's compliance
    language marks takes them all off. The piece keeps its type and decision; Undo takes a mark back.
  - The marked words come out in the compliance style (bold by default, Settings sets it) in the Excel matrix, the
    Word outline, the coverage report and the setup documents. The matrix keeps the marks (a hidden column).
  - The piece pane shows the piece's words with its compliance language in that style, so what the list catches is
    visible while you shred.
  - H1 to H9 everywhere a level is named (Nicholas 18:11): the Setup builder's level buttons (H1, H2, H3, H4 to H9...),
    its right-click Make it, the tags and tips; Shred's outline adds + H1 at the target's level (+ H3 after an H3) and
    the section right-click says + H3 after it, + H4 under it. The customer's structure (Fix text > Heading, 4.17.0)
    and ours use the same words. Word's style names stay Heading 1 to 9.

DOCUMENTS: REPLACE A FILE, DELETE INTO THE TRASH; HEADINGS WITH LEVELS (4.17.0)
  - A document's right-click (and its More): Replace the file... puts the picked file in place of the document's
    file, under the same name. It stays the same document and the same version (no Amendment 1), and the shred reads
    it again: pieces whose sentence stayed keep every decision, a changed sentence keeps its piece and decisions with
    the new words (marked Changed when the file was replaced), new sentences arrive as new pieces, and pieces whose
    sentence is gone go to the Trash. The old file goes to the Trash too.
  - A file edited where it sits in Customer documents: the right-click says Read it again, and adding it again offers
    "The same document, edited: replace the file" (chosen for you), not a new version.
  - Delete... (a document) and Delete this version... (a version's right-click) ask first, naming the versions, files
    and shred pieces that go with it, with Cancel the default. Everything moves to the effort's Trash; Undo (Ctrl+Z on
    Documents) puts the last one back. Documents > More > Trash... lists what was deleted or replaced, with Restore and
    Empty the Trash... (which asks, and is the only thing that removes files for good).
  - Shred > Fix text > Heading: H1 to H9, or Not a heading. A heading gives every piece
    below it, up to the next heading, its number and title (the matrix's Section and Section title). A heading with no
    number reads under the section it is in ("L.2 Proposal Organization > Volume I"), or under the headings marked
    above it. Start from > the RFP's own outline takes each marked heading in order, at its level, with its page.
  - Shred says when the file open is an older version whose changed pieces wait in Documents > Changes.

REDLINE: COMMENTS FROM THE PDF (4.16.0)
  - Make the Word copy brings in the comments reviewers left on the PDF in Acrobat, each on exactly the words it marks,
    under the reviewer's name and date:
      a cross-out is a tracked deletion of the struck letters (part of a word stays part of a word);
      an insert is a tracked insertion at the letter the caret marks;
      Replace text is a tracked deletion and insertion in one place;
      a highlight or underline is a Word comment on its words (its note, or Highlighted when it has none);
      replies stay replies, and a comment set to Completed is marked done.
  - A sticky note, text box, callout, shape or stamp has no words of its own: it goes on the words nearest it (a
    callout on the words its line points at, a shape on the words inside it) and Check says it was placed by position.
    A mark on a running header goes on the page's first words.
  - Check still holds the copy to the PDF word for word (with the comments' changes undone) and lists every comment
    under the differences: what it became, the words it is on, the reviewer and the page.
  - Exceptions reads the copy's comments as rows too, each with its words and its replies.
  - More > Make it without the PDF's comments makes a clean copy.

REDLINE FIDELITY: SLIDES AND TABLES WITH NO BORDERS (4.15.0)
  - A deck saved as PDF (PowerPoint or another slide program) becomes slides in the Word copy: one page a slide, the
    slide's size. The slide's pictures, charts, shapes and table shading sit behind the page as one picture; every box
    of text sits where it was, in its size, weight and color, bullets and levels as written. The text stays editable
    with Track Changes and comments, and Check and Exceptions read it like any other text. Each slide reads title
    first, then top to bottom and left to right.
  - Prepare knows a deck from the program that saved it. More > Make it as slides, or Make it as a document, decides
    for a PDF the other way.
  - A table with no borders and no tags takes its columns from the gaps no word crosses, so a column of right-aligned
    numbers lines up by its ends. A table drawn inside a cell stays inside that cell.
  - Text that is part of a picture (a pasted screenshot, a chart saved as an image) stays in the picture.

REDLINE FIDELITY: TAGS AND TABLES ACROSS PAGES (4.14.0)
  - When Word saved the PDF with its accessibility tags (Save as PDF does by default), Redline reads them: a table
    with no borders and a table inside another table's cell come out as Word tables, cell for cell. A PDF printed to
    PDF or flattened has no tags; its tables still come from their borders.
  - A table that runs across pages is one table in the copy. The header row the PDF repeats on each page is kept once
    and set to repeat, as Word does; Check leaves the repeats out too.
  - Inside a cell, a list or a second paragraph stays its own paragraph; turned header text stays turned.

REDLINE FIDELITY: TABLES FROM THEIR BORDERS (4.13.0)
  - Redline's copy reads a table from the borders the PDF draws (lines, and the thin filled bars Word uses), not only
    from text that lines up. Cells merged across or down come out merged, an empty cell stays empty, a cell whose text
    wraps stays one cell, shading and a bold or shaded header row are kept (the header repeats on each page), a table
    with horizontal rules only takes its columns from the gaps between the words, and turned header text reads as
    written. A table with no borders still comes from text that lines up.
  - The copy follows the page: each page's size and orientation (a landscape page stays landscape), the margins, the
    body font and size, the space above each paragraph, line to line spacing, indents, centered and justified text, a
    new page where the PDF starts one, and headings in the size, weight and color the PDF set them in.
  - Check reads each table the way the copy holds it, so a faithful copy still shows no difference.

CAPTURE CARDS AND DELIVERABLES (4.12.0)
  - On Shred, Capture > Contact, Key date or Effort detail opens a card at the top of the piece pane, not a dialog. It
    stays open while you read: pick words on any page (drag across them), then Take on the field they belong in. What
    the first piece says fills the blank fields; Take and typing replace what a field holds.
  - A contact holds several phones, e-mails and addresses, each with a label (Office, Mobile, Fax, Main, Other or a
    word of your own) and the page it came from. The card's top row picks New contact or one already filed: a filed
    contact shows every value to fix in place, and new picks add to it. Save files the card and marks every piece it
    took words from; Cancel drops it. One card at a time: opening another asks to save or discard the open one.
  - Captured from this piece, in the pane: each capture with Open (a contact opens on the card) and Take out (a
    contact loses only what that piece gave it).
  - A piece typed Deliverable shows Which deliverable: the deliverables on the effort's board. Picking one attaches the
    piece; its page lists it under From the solicitation (Requirements tab). With none on the board, the drop-down says
    No deliverables yet and a button opens Dashboard > Deliverables, where deliverables are added.
  - Delete a deliverable: Delete this deliverable... on its page, a board card's right-click, or Delete... in the List
    view. It asks first (Cancel is the default) and says what goes with it. Undo (Ctrl+Z on the Deliverables page)
    puts it back with everything it held, until Stationkeeper closes; the effort record keeps a copy either way.
  - Redline moved in the menu: it sits under Documents, indented (its tabs Prepare, Check and Exceptions as before).
  - Fixed: a rare close of the app with no message just as a background job finished (Full Text, Read, adding
    documents and four other places let go of the job's thread a moment too early when the PC was busy).

REDLINE TABLES (4.11.0)
  - Redline's Word copy makes real Word tables. A PDF keeps no tables, only text placed on the page: rows whose pieces
    line up in columns become a table, its column widths from where the columns sit, and a cell that wrapped onto a
    second line stays in its cell. A list or numbered headings with a gap after the marker stay paragraphs.
  - Check compares the table's words cell by cell, as the copy holds them, and says how many tables it made: look at
    each beside the PDF (a merged cell or a cell with no text can come out differently).

THE MORNING'S ANSWERS (4.10.0)
  - Suggestion rules: the shred's phrase rules are one growing team list, the Suggestion rules sheet of Company
    Standards.xlsx (the phrase, what it suggests, why, Approved or Retired). Times kept and Times not right count every
    effort on every PC that shares the team library. On Shred, Not right beside a suggestion dismisses it for that piece
    only; Make it a team rule... on a sorted piece adds its phrase to the list. A Retired rule stays listed, unused.
  - Theme: the workbook's Theme sheet holds Word's twelve theme colors and the headings and body fonts. A standard with
    no theme starts from the Office colors and fonts when you fill the sheet in.
  - An effort's workbook has a Volume column: blank means every volume; a volume kind's name means that volume only.
  - Settings > Team library: Read it now reads what changed without waiting for the two-minute check.
  - The library's Word files carry plain names in the team library (Cover.docx, Cover (2).docx); Library files
    index.csv beside them says which file the app keeps for each.
  - Effort data for the macros: the first snapshot builds on its own thread (no pause when an effort opens), and the
    manifest's CUI flag follows the effort's marking (Not CUI and Proprietary say false; any other marking, or none,
    says true).

EFFORT DATA FOR STATIONKEEPER MACROS (4.9.0)
  - The app keeps a read-only snapshot of each effort's rules in the effort's folder, Stationkeeper Data, for the Word
    macros (Stationkeeper Macros): manifest.json, rules\rules.json (styles allowed, table types, placeholders, banned
    and avoided phrases, fonts, the acronyms in the list and the terms that are not, page limits, caption settings),
    templates\effort.dotx (the effort's template, no macros) and dictionaries\effort.dic (the team's dictionaries and
    the effort's acronyms, as Word writes dictionaries). Do not edit it: edit the effort here.
  - It is published within seconds of a change and when an effort with no snapshot opens. Only the parts that changed
    are written, each under a temporary name and then renamed, the manifest last, so the macros never read half a file.
    The revision only goes up; rules.json carries the manifest's revision. The template is rebuilt whenever its
    styles, table looks or Quick Parts change. The macros call a snapshot Aging after 24 hours and Stale after 72.
  - While Word has the effort's template open (a document attached to it), the rules and the dictionary still go out
    and the template is written on a later try, a minute apart; the manifest keeps the one Word has.
  - Nothing is written outside the effort's folder. The rules follow a JSON Schema the app carries
    (rules\effort-data.schema.json beside them).

REDLINE (4.8.0)
  - A menu item of its own, below the six steps and with no number: redlining happens at any point (the draft RFP, Q&A,
    the final RFP's terms, amendments, a model contract). Authors redline in Word with Track Changes; there is no
    editor here. Every role sees it. Its files go to the effort's Redlines folder.
  - Prepare: Make the Word copy makes a clean Word copy of a customer PDF from the text the shred reads, with Fix
    text's fixes: real Heading styles from the customer's headings (L.3.5.2 is Heading 4), the numbers typed exactly as
    the customer wrote them (never Word's automatic numbering), paragraphs joined across lines and pages, and the
    running headers, footers and page numbers left out. A list item keeps its marker as text. Another PDF... (under
    More) copies a file outside the solicitation, such as a model contract. Fix text in Shred first if a page needs it.
  - Check: Check the copy compares it with its PDF word for word and lists every word added, dropped or changed, with
    its page and the heading above it; a line set aside in Fix text is listed as set aside on purpose. Check the
    returned redline... reads the redline the authors sent back with every tracked change rejected: any difference is
    an edit made with Track Changes off, which Word cannot show. Double-click a finding to see it on the page in Shred.
  - Exceptions: Read the redline's changes... makes a row of every tracked change: the customer's number (the heading
    above it), the original text, our text, Inserted, Deleted or Replaced, the author, the date and the comment on it.
    Export to Excel or Word.
  - Documents: the panel says when a redline copy was made, and a PDF's right-click offers Prepare a redline copy.
  - Limits: a scanned page is only as good as its reading.

THE TEAM'S LISTS AS FILES (4.7.1)
  - The small lists are sheets of Company Standards.xlsx: Phrases (banned and avoided, with what to use instead and
    why), Authors (and the efforts each writes for, or Every effort), Compliance words, Piece types (names, keys,
    colors, shown) and Document types (each with its initial). Change them in Excel or in the app; each side follows.
  - The large lists are files of their own in the team library's Lists folder: Acronyms.csv is the master acronym list
    (the same file as Stationkeeper Data\acronym_database.csv), and each custom dictionary is a Word dictionary,
    Lists\Dictionaries\<name>.dic, which Word can use as it is. A word added in either place reaches the other.
  - Faster: with many document versions, moving between pages no longer waits on the menu's count of changes (each
    pair of versions is compared once), and Changes draws its page view only when you turn it on.

THE TEAM LIBRARY AS FILES (4.7.0)
  - The global standards are now called the Company Standards (Default), everywhere.
  - Their values live in one Excel workbook, Company Standards.xlsx, in the team library (Stationkeeper Data\Team library
    unless Settings > The team > Team library points it at another folder, such as a SharePoint library synced to
    each PC). Edit it in Excel or in the app: the app reads what changed (every two minutes and when it opens) and
    writes what it changes. While Excel has it open, the app waits and says so.
  - Sheets: Styles, Table looks, Table types, Heading numbering, List numbering, Page setups, Captions, Fonts allowed,
    Settings (the base fonts, sizes and margins), Fields (the field recipes), About, and Columns (what each column holds).
  - One row per item. First the organizing columns: Name, Category, Status, Changed (who, when, in the app or in Excel),
    Used on, Set, Menu group, Show in menu, Notes, Tags, Owner (and a hidden ID, so a renamed row keeps its values).
    Then only the property columns some row uses, in groups that collapse (Font, Paragraph, Spacing, Indents, Borders
    and shading, Numbering, Other), and Other Word settings last. Only Approved rows reach templates and the macros;
    Draft and Retired rows wait in the workbook. Columns you add are kept both ways and never reach Word.
  - A property column you take out asks once: take it off every row, or keep the values.
  - The Word files (covers, back covers, title pages, Quick Parts sources, header and footer designs) sit beside the
    workbook in Content. Edit them in Word; the app's copies follow, and a file the app keeps appears there. A file
    changed in both places keeps the newer and says so. Nothing is deleted.
  - Each effort has its own workbook beside its acronym list, <effort> Effort Formatting.xlsx, with the same sheets and
    only that effort's changes: a blank cell means the Company Standards (Default) value. Effort Formatting > Open this
    effort's workbook opens it.
  - A copy of the workbook from another PC ("Company Standards-PCNAME.xlsx") is reported, never read. A workbook that
    cannot be read is reported and skipped; the last good reading stays.
  - Field recipes show what a field gives while you type it, on a sample page (page 3 of 12, under "2 Technical
    Approach"): PAGE, NUMPAGES, SECTIONPAGES, SEQ with its restart, STYLEREF, DOCPROPERTY, the dates with their pictures,
    and what a TOC lists. A caption recipe reads "Table 2-2". Other fields say Word shows them; Check in Word updates
    every field in a real copy. The Fields sheet's Sample result is filled the same way.

ADDING STARTS THE SHRED (4.2.0)
  - Add a customer document in Documents and it is extracted for the shred in the background, whatever its role; you
    never pick it again in Shred. Left out documents are not. A new version is extracted at once too: the sentences
    that stay the same keep their pieces and decisions (on the new version's pages), new sentences arrive as new pieces,
    and changed ones wait in Documents > Changes. Nothing appears twice.
  - The compliance matrix saves itself. Each day it changes, a copy goes to Matrix history beside it, named with the
    date ({Effort}_Compliance_Matrix_YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx). Every copy is kept.
  - Fix text is a mode inside Shred (F2, or Fix text on Shred's bar): Combine (J), Split (S), Heading, Set aside (a page
    header, footer or banner; More: every line like it), Add missed text, Suspects (F3) and Page checked (Ctrl+Enter,
    "37 of 120 pages checked"). It never holds the shred up. Documents has no Text check tab; the page-by-page check is
    under Fix text > More.
  - A sentence the page break split is joined and marked "Joined across p.N to N+1"; a page number caught inside it is
    taken out. Suspects walks through the joins (Keep the join, Undo the join), pages that end mid-sentence, fragments,
    words broken across a line and characters the reader could not read.
  - Shred's page scrolls through every page and draws only the pages in view, so long documents open fast. A drag held
    at the top or bottom of the page scrolls on, so a selection can run across a page break. Page Up and Page Down go
    a page at a time; Fix text > More > One page at a time fits a page in the view.

SHRED (4.1.0)
  - Shred says what each piece of the customer's text is. The bar names the types: Requirement (R), Instruction (L),
    Rule (U), Date (D), Evaluation (E), Deliverable (V), Information (G), Support (C), Not applicable (X). Each piece
    keeps its type (the matrix workbook's Piece column); Undo takes the type back with the decision.
  - Pick a customer document in Shred's list: one not extracted yet is extracted at once, in the background, and opens
    when it is done (its progress and Stop show on Shred). There is no Extract tab. Mark shredded when a document is done.
  - Shred's tabs: Requirements, Rules & Dates (the Dashboard's Submission Rules, moved, with the shred's Rule and Date
    pieces, each with where it came from), Evaluation (moved from Matrix & Outline) and Acros & Abs. The Acro List
    opens from Acros & Abs: The list > More (the whole list, Lists to submit, Import a list) and Export.
  - The Q&A log is a drawer: the Q&A button (with the count not answered) on Shred, Matrix & Outline and Drafts opens it
    beside the page. Asking a question on a piece opens it on that question. Documents has no Customer Q&A tab.
  - Documents > Changes decides each change three ways: No impact, Re-shred (into Shred's queue: "N to re-shred", each
    passage with its old and new wording) or Ask a question (citing both wordings). The full list is under More.
  - Documents' panel adds a document's pieces (how many not sorted) and its acros and abs (found, to decide).
  - The word-drag tool on Shred's page is Make a sentence (Shift+S), so "extract" means only pulling a document in.

    Dashboard as the map, then Team and Settings.
  - Documents: the solicitation package, complete, current and readable. Click a document: the panel says what has been
    done to it (its text checked, the shred, the changes from its last version, whether it feeds the matrix, its

WHY IT RUNS WITHOUT AN ADMINISTRATOR PASSWORD
  - Windows asks for an admin password only when a program declares that it needs one, or when it writes to protected,
    machine-wide places: Program Files, the Windows folder, the machine-wide registry, services and drivers. Installers
    ask because they write there.
  - Stationkeeper has no installer. Its exe declares "run as the person who started me" (asInvoker), and it writes only
    inside its own folder, which you own. Windows has nothing to ask permission for.
  - The Windows sandbox is not the reason. Only the OCR reader (Tesseract) runs in a Windows AppContainer, and that
    takes rights away: no network, no files beyond two folders. A sandbox cannot grant or skip admin rights.
  - Not an approval: it can read and write what you can, as any program you run can. Its protection comes from having no
    network code (checked at every build), the no-network sandbox for the OCR reader, pinned and checked components
    (SBOM, known-advisory check), read-only documents and signed update packs.
  - Running does not mean approved. It runs because this PC does not block programs in your own folders (AppLocker or
    WDAC); IT can turn that on at any time. Give IT security SECURITY.md, the SBOM and the checksums.
  - The full explanation is in SECURITY.md (section 10) and in Settings > About and security.

SECOND READER: docTR (from v2.1.0)
  Every picture is now read twice: by Tesseract and by docTR, a neural text finder and reader (Mindee, France,
  Apache 2.0) running on ONNX Runtime (Microsoft, MIT). docTR finds text Tesseract misses: labels inside colored
  boxes, words touching diagram lines, photographed whiteboards, busy backgrounds, faint or low-resolution text.
  - In a test of 13 hard diagrams (146 labels), Tesseract alone found 56%; Tesseract + docTR found 97%.
  - Where Tesseract already read a word, its reading stands. docTR adds text Tesseract never found, and a longer
    reading only when it is at least 95% sure (TWTA where Tesseract read WTA). Nothing is counted twice.
  - Extra passes: large pictures are read in tiles, dark pictures also inverted, and pages with vertical labels
    also turned 90 and 270 degrees.
  - Cost: about 1 second more per picture (several seconds for very large drawings). Typed text is not affected.
  - The models were exported by this project from Mindee's official weights (vendor\doctr\SOURCE.txt has every
    SHA-256). They load from the app's own folder; nothing is downloaded.
  - To turn it off: set "doctr_enabled": 0 in config\ocr_settings.json.

     again, with that term outlined in yellow. Typed-text results have nothing to check.
  2. Fix what is wrong: correct the spelling and pick "Save as text", or mark "Not text". Click Save Choices.
  3. Improve Reading (top bar, or the green bar when corrections pile up) opens one window with one
     action: Teach and Apply. A progress bar walks through 4 steps (prepare lines, measure the current
     reader, teach, measure the new reader). It shows the score before and after, for example
     "Words read correctly: 50% before, 93% after", plus lines it now reads correctly.
     It becomes your reader ONLY if it reads better. "Put back the previous reader" undoes it.
  4. Rescan when it offers (only files with pictures are read again).
  5. Share: "Share with team..." saves a Knowledge Pack (your reader + database + OCR fixes + ignore list)
     for colleagues to install. Improve Reading = better reader on THIS PC. Knowledge Pack = share it.
  Rescans after Improve Reading re-read only files that contain pictures; the rest reuse saved results.

WORDS AT THE END OF A SENTENCE (v1.8.0)
  "award." or "system." at a sentence end is no longer reported as an abbreviation: ordinary English words
  (a built-in list of common words, SCOWL) are skipped. Real abbreviations are still reported, including
  ones that are also words, such as "approx.", "Fig.", "No.", "Ref.", "temp.", "spec.". An all-caps term
  before a period ("CCB.") is reported once, as the acronym. Anything else wrong: select it and Exclude.

WHAT THE TRAINER SHOWS YOU
  Only what the scanner has to OCR: pictures inside Word, PowerPoint, and Excel files (including
  vector diagrams), image files, and PDF pages (shown whole, since a scanned page is one picture).
  Typed text on a slide or page is read exactly from the file, letter for letter, so it never needs
  training and never appears in the Trainer.

CANCEL A SCAN
  "Cancel Scan" (under the status line, shown while scanning) stops at the next page or slide.
  The table returns to the results from before that scan. In a project, files already read are kept,
  so the next rescan continues from there.

CLIENT ACRONYM LISTS (from an RFP or the customer)
  "Client Lists" adds the client's acronym list: CSV, TXT, Excel, Word, or PDF (a two-column table or
  lines such as "SOW - Statement of Work"). You see a preview of the pairs read before it is added.
  Client definitions take precedence over the database for defining and choosing between meanings:
    Purple row, "Client list"                    = found term matches the client list
    Pink row, "* Client list (database differs)" = the database has a different meaning too
  Every meaning appears in the choice list labeled [Client] or [Database]: pick either.
  Status filters "Client list" and "Client and database differ" show just those rows.
  With no client lists added, scans work exactly as before. In a project, the lists are saved with it.
  Client lists stay on this PC and in the project file: never in Knowledge Packs or GitHub.

FIRST USE OF EACH ACRONYM (where to define it)
  The "First Use (acronyms)" column shows, for each acronym, where it first appears in each file
  (reading order: pages, slides, Word sections, then headers/footers and pictures) and whether that
  first use is written the proposal way: Spelled Out Words (ACR).
    check defined         = first use is "Words (ACR)"
    X defined later       = first use is bare; "(ACR)" appears later. Move the definition up.
    X not defined         = never written as "(ACR)" in that file
    ? check               = found through an OCR correction; look at it yourself
  Each file counts on its own, since each volume must define its own acronyms.
  Units and abbreviations never get a First Use entry.
  Status > "Not defined at first use" lists every acronym to fix. Double-click a row for every file.
  "First found / Last found by a scan" (projects only) are scan dates, not places in the document.

EQUATIONS AND SYMBOLS (from v2.0.0)
  Every scan also finds equations and checks their symbols.
  - Word and PowerPoint equation-editor math is read exactly: subscripts, Greek letters, accents
    (m-dot, v-bar, x-hat), fractions, roots and sums.
  - Typed math in any file ("q = ½ ρ V²", "σ_b = M_b · c / I_x") is found in the text.
  - Equations in pictures are read by OCR, listed for review as "CHECK EQUATION PICTURE", and shown
    in the preview pane. OCR reads letters well but can miss subscripts, Greek letters and fraction bars.
  Each symbol gets one row with a status:
    Defined in document    a "where ṁ is the mass flow rate" sentence, a nomenclature list, or a
                           Symbol/Meaning table defines it
    SYMBOL NOT DEFINED     no file and no database entry defines it; fix before submission
  Each equation row lists its symbols and which ones are not defined. Status filters
  "Equations and symbols" and "Undefined symbols" show them. Select a symbol to see its meaning and
  every equation that uses it. "TRL = 6" and "cost = $4.2M" are not treated as equations.

EQUATION PICTURES (2.55.0; the math reader add-on is gone)
  Equation pictures are read by the text reader (ML, on this PC), which learns math and unit characters (Greek,
  operators, degrees, micro, ohm). Position rules turn smaller lowered characters into subscripts, raised ones into
  superscripts, and a short line between text into a fraction (a/b). No generative AI is in the program.

SYMBOL PICKER (from v2.3.0)
  Type Greek letters, subscripts, superscripts, math operators, accents and units without leaving the app.
  - Click the Ω button next to a correction field (preview pane, Trainer, the Definition / Fix OCR field,
    Add Item, OCR Fixes), right-click any of those fields > Symbols..., or press Ctrl+Shift+Space.
  - The Symbols window stays open and inserts into the field you clicked last, so you can fix several readings.
  - Tabs: Recent, Greek, Subscript, Superscript, Operators, Accents, Units, Arrows/sets.
  - Search by name: "sigma", "dot", "sub 2", "degree", "ohm".
  - Accents attach to the letter before the cursor: type m, click the dot accent, get ṁ.
  - x₂ Sub / x² Sup / Normal turn the selected text (or the character before the cursor) into subscript,
    superscript or normal text: select "sp" in "Isp", click Sub, get Iₛₚ. Also on the right-click menu.

FULL TEXT MODE (from v2.0.0)

ONEDRIVE AND SHAREPOINT

  OneDrive's own traffic inside your organization's Microsoft 365, not this app's. For a big

  so everyone with access to that folder sees them (terms and file names, no document text).

TRAINER (a window inside Stationkeeper.exe: Advanced > Trainer, or right-click > Check in picture)
  Not included in the standard AcronymHunter_Portable.zip. Open a file, drag a box around text in a
  picture. Each line OCR found gets a number (matching the box on the picture) and a color:
    GREEN = the scanner keeps this reading (it can appear in results).
    RED   = low confidence; the scanner already ignores it.
  Pick one choice per line, then click Save Choices:
    Skip          Leave it out.
    Save as text  It is real text. Fix the spelling in the box first (typing picks this choice).
    Not text      Diagram lines, arrows, or specks read as letters or Asian characters. Teaches the
                  model that spot is background. If the line was GREEN, that exact reading also goes on
                  config\ocr_ignore.csv so the scanner drops it from pictures on the next scan.
                  Typed document text is never affected. "Ignore List" opens the file to review.
  "Add OCR Fixes from Corrections" makes the scanner fix a misread word right away.
  "Undo Last Save" removes everything the last save added, including ignore-list entries.
  "How training works" explains how samples become a better model. "Prepare Training Package"
  gets them ready for training (TRAINING_GUIDE.md section 8).
  Samples go to training_data\ in this folder (crops of your documents; handle them accordingly).

DATABASE FORMAT (acronym_database.csv)
  Term,Definition,Category,Action
  SOW,Statement of Work,Acronym,Include
  CEO,Chief Executive Officer,Acronym,Exclude
  Columns can be in any order. The app finds them by header name and keeps your order, your header
  names, and any extra columns (Notes, Source...) when it saves. Accepted header names:
    Term: Term, Item, Acronym, Abbreviation   Definition: Definition, Meaning, Description, Expansion
    Category: Category, Type                   Action: Action, Rule, Include/Exclude
  Close the CSV in Excel before clicking Include or Exclude in the app.
  Symbols (°, µ, Ω, Δ, Cyrillic, math letters) are fully supported. In Excel, save with
  File > Save As > "CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited)". Avoid plain "CSV (Comma delimited)": it turns
  symbols into "?". The app also reads "Unicode Text" (UTF-16) files, and re-saves them as UTF-8.

VERIFY YOUR COPY (optional)
  About Stationkeeper\CHECKSUM.txt holds the exe fingerprint. In PowerShell:
    Get-FileHash .\Stationkeeper.exe
  The Hash must match CHECKSUM.txt and the value the sender gave you.
  The app is not code-signed. Right-click > Properties > Details shows the publisher and version.
