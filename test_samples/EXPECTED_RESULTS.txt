ACRONYM HUNTER: TEST SAMPLES
============================
All content is fictional ("Kestrel" smallsat program, "Northwind Orbital").
CUI and ITAR markings in the Word file are fake test markings.

HOW TO TEST
  1. Open AcronymHunter.exe.
  2. Click "Scan Folder" and pick this test_samples folder (or "Select Files" for one file).
  3. Compare the table against the checks below.
  A full scan of all 7 files takes about 1 to 2 minutes (OCR is the slow part).
  Baseline from 2026-10-20: 98 terms found, 1 file error.


THE FILES
---------

Kestrel_Technical_Volume.docx   Word
  Tests: body text, a table, an embedded figure (OCR), header and footer, OCR typo line.
  Expect: SOW (Resolved), RFP, CDR and LEO (CHOOSE DEFINITION), psi, kWh, approx., w/o.
          SOW location tagged "[OCR fix: S0W]" (confusion-map correction).
          GSFC and EPS found from text inside the embedded Figure 4 image.
          TBD, CEO, USA, II suppressed (visible only with "Show Excluded").
  Known gaps: CUI and ITAR live in the header/footer and are NOT found.
              DoD is NOT found (mixed-case acronyms are missed).
              RFF is NOT corrected to RFP (F/P is not a known OCR confusion).
              MIL-STD-882E splits into MIL and STD.

Kestrel_Program_Manual.pdf   PDF, 8 pages
  Tests: digital text pages (1 to 6), an embedded figure on page 6, and two image-only
         scanned pages (7 and 8) that need full-page OCR.
  Expect: IMS at p.7, GEVS, torr, Grms, RFAs from the scanned pages. SOWs (plural) counted as SOW.
          VCRM, SRD, WBS, MEOP as new candidates.
  Known gaps: OCR on page 8 can drop short lines (CO, QA). Check p.8 hits in the Locations column.
              ALL-CAPS headings (SAFETY, ORBIT, POWER) appear as noise candidates.

Kestrel_PDR_Briefing.pptx   PowerPoint, 5 slides
  Tests: title and bullet text, a table, two pictures (OCR), a grouped text box, speaker notes.
  Expect: C&DH, GNC, COPV, TRL from the table. ADCS, TT&C, I/F, GHz from the diagram image on slide 5.
  Known gaps: MOC and LEOP (grouped callout, slide 5) and QA and FRR (speaker notes) are NOT found.

Kestrel_Compliance_Matrix.xlsx   Excel, 2 sheets
  Tests: text cells on two sheets, numeric cells (skipped on purpose), an embedded image (OCR).
  Expect: KES, COPV, MEOP, EOL, TT&C, SOW, RFP, psi, kWh, w/o. SE&I from the image and Risk Register.
          Sheet name appears in Locations, e.g. "(Risk Register)".
  Known gaps: "Add" and "Tank" show as unit candidates (word after a number). Noise.

Kestrel_System_Diagram.png   Clean image
  Tests: OCR on a crisp block diagram.
  Expect: C&DH, EPS, ADCS, TT&C, GNC, ICD, I/F, plus SOW and RFP from the caption line.

Kestrel_Whiteboard_Photo.jpg   Noisy, tilted photo
  Tests: image cleanup (contrast, denoise) before OCR on a low-quality picture.
  Expect: CDR, ICD, IMS, LEO, PDR, SRR, TBD, TRL, psi, w/o, approx.
          If these drop out after a code change, the image cleanup step broke.

Corrupt_File_Test.pdf   Broken file
  Tests: error isolation. One bad file must not stop the batch.
  Expect: a row "Corrupt_File_Test.pdf", status "Could not read file". All other files still scanned.
          logs\error.log appears in the app folder with the details.


WHAT TO TRY IN THE APP
  - Select CDR, pick "Critical Design Review", click Apply Choice.
  - Select COPV, type "Composite Overwrapped Pressure Vessel", click Include in DB. Rescan: it shows as Resolved.
  - Select SCOPE, click Exclude. Rescan: it disappears unless "Show Excluded" is checked.
  - Click Export CSV and open the report.
  Note: Include and Exclude change acronym_database.csv in the app folder you are running.


KNOWN GAPS THESE FILES EXPOSE (from the architecture review)
  F4  Mixed-case (DoD) and hyphenated (MIL-STD-882E) acronyms
  F5  Sentence-ending words flagged as abbreviations (award., model., panel.)
  F6  ALL-CAPS headings flagged as acronyms (SCOPE, OF, WORK, APPENDIX)
  F8  Word headers/footers, PowerPoint notes and grouped shapes not read
  After fixing any of these, rescan this folder and confirm the gap closes and nothing
  in the "Expect" lines disappears.
