Company logos built into Stationkeeper (3.0.3).

Put the company logo here as logo_01.png (and logo_02.png, logo_03.png for teammates, in order), PNG on a clear or white
background, at most about 600 pixels wide. The build bundles this folder into the program, and the logos show at the top
of the menu on every PC. Nobody can add or change a logo from inside the app.
